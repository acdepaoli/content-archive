Think about your last vendor negotiation.

You had terms ready. Benchmarks. A walk-away position if it went sideways.

Now think about your last benefits renewal.

One was a negotiation. The other was a presentation. (You know which was which.)

You built this company by never taking the first offer. Freight, software, banking, raw materials, every major contract gets scrutiny and a real alternative on the table.

Then the health plan shows up as a number, not a conversation, and nobody blinks.

For most companies your size, that spend sits behind only payroll.

Every dollar a misaligned vendor pulls out of your health plan is a tax on what you built.

The renewal is the one room you keep walking out of without leverage. Somebody told you that was fine.

It wasn't. And how is that working for you?
