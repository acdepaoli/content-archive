Every raise at your company gets approved. Someone proposes it. Finance models it. Someone signs.

Except one line.

Every year, your health plan reprices the cost of employing every person on your payroll. No proposal. No sign-off. A renewal letter shows up and the number is different. (Higher. It's always higher.)

A plan trending 8% a year doubles in nine. Nobody in your building approved that. Everybody in your building pays for it, you in margin, your people in deductions that eat their raises.

And we treat it like weather. A 12% jump gets a groan and a "see you next year." Your freight vendor tries that and you're in their office by lunch.

Your workforce got repriced this year.

Were you part of the decision, or just on the distribution list?
